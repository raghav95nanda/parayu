# Word-bank data attribution

The curated vocabulary arrangement, short learning meanings, and original teaching examples in `vocabulary-source.txt`, `example-overrides.txt`, `compiled-vocabulary.json`, and the generated `dist/vocabulary-data.json` are offered under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International:

The curated selection is informed by written-corpus frequency, not a claim of the universally most frequent 1,000 spoken words. Source ranks and counts, where available, are retained per entry.

Frequency data: Kavya Manohar, Malayalam Word Frequency Distribution, derived from the AI4Bharat Indic NLP corpus. Dataset license: CC BY-SA 3.0.

https://huggingface.co/datasets/kavyamanohar/ml-word-frequency
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Dictionary cross-references and spelling checks, where available: English Wiktionary contributors, via Tatu Ylonen's Kaikki / Wiktextract extraction. Wiktionary data is available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Individual entries retain their English Wiktionary URLs. Original concise learning meanings and sentences were composed for this app; raw dictionary definitions and corpus texts are not distributed.

https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Malayalam/index.html
https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights

The original teaching examples and word selection have been adapted for a speaking-first travel learning experience. The Latin transliteration is an approximate reading aid. The separately generated audio uses the Piper Arjun Malayalam voice, whose source is recorded in the audio manifests and README.
